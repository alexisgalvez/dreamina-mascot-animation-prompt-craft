User Manual: Mascot Prompt Craft

1 Prepare & Upload Mascot

Export your vector mascot artwork from your design software (Illustrator, CorelDRAW, Figma, etc.) as a transparent PNG or SVG. Upload it in the @Image 1 box. If you upload an SVG, the tool will automatically extract and display your brand color palette!

2 Select Style & Environment

Choose a 3D rendering style (such as Pixar 3D, Cel-Shaded Anime, or Claymation) and select an environment setting. Volumetric and aesthetic tokens will automatically be woven into your generated prompts.

3 Craft the Storyboard Timeline

Use the timeline builder to structure the mascot's narrative beats. For each beat, select quick preset parameters or pick "Other / Custom Text..." to type your own character actions, expressions, camera movements, or fx.

4 Compile & Copy Prompts

Switch between the tabs to copy either the **Seedance 2.5 Video Prompt** or the **Seedream 5.0 Canvas Prompt**. Click the copy button to copy it instantly.

5 Generate in Dreamina

Open dreamina.capcut.com, upload your mascot image as @Image 1 (set reference weight slider in Dreamina to match your lock weight), paste the copied prompt, and generate!
